ABOUT REUNITE

Reconnecting Families, Restoring Hope

ReUnite is a Family Reunification System designed to help people register, search and reconnect with their missing or separated family members during disasters and emergencies.

OUR PURPOSE

Helping People Find Their Loved Ones

During natural disasters, emergencies and displacement, families can become separated from each other.

ReUnite provides a centralized platform where essential information about separated persons can be registered and searched easily.

Our goal is to make the process of finding family members faster, simpler and more organized.

Our Mission

No family should remain separated when technology can help bring them together.

One Platform
